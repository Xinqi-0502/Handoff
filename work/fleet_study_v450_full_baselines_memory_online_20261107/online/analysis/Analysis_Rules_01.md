# Pre-outcome independent online audit and aggregation rules

Frozen before any V450 online policy-performance pilot or64-case result. The independent script uses only Python standard-library readers. It neither imports nor calls the solver, forecast, production audit or SUMO. Its input paths are explicit; no latest-run discovery or outcome-based result selection is permitted.

1. Audit the entire planned matrix:27 policy calls per case, two fixed pilot cases or all64 cases from eight mothers. Check common initializer, all9 arm/budget cells,3 technical repetitions, source/input/gate identity, ordered trace timestamps, full executable action sequence at each improvement, literal deadlines, returned unweighted certificates, fallback flags, activation tick and0.2s quantization.
2. Reconstruct the exact native selection rule from raw policy rows. Each distinct selected plan keeps its group-median-latency repetition (lower middle for two); every plan remains. Replays may be shared only for identical case+plan+activation tick. The representative selected first in the deterministic schedule supplies a shared replay's input latency; each linked selection still retains its own raw/rounded latency.
3. Independently replay event bookkeeping, not simulation: original cargo ownership, initial weight12, first weight1 delivered at6s, every old/new delivery once, correct receiver, capacity at every transfer, atomic transfer order, forced a delivery before b acceptance, all4s services and2s handoffs. Check these intervals against every relevant native frame. Recompute all-customer/old/new flow, supplier waiting/dwell, hash the canonical JSON trace, and verify vehicle/person trip completion and collision output.
4. Keep last handoff, truck release command, first observed motion and first observed departure from the hub separate. Check the actual signal movement and pedestrian proximity/exposure counters from the trace. Proximity is not a causal delay estimate. Forecast predictions begin at decision time; native execution includes measured planning delay. A forecast/actual rank reversal can therefore include both traffic-model and activation-delay differences.
5. Primary all-case physical aggregate: **policy-frequency-weighted representative replay J**. Weight each retained plan's actual representative replay by its number of represented policy repetitions divided by3. Never call it a mean of3 native repetitions. Also retain plan count, every representative row, and its actual-J range. If any representative is missing or failed, the complete case/arm/budget physical aggregate is missing; never renormalize surviving plans.
6. Preserve every planned scenario in paired tables. Report known/unknown counts, both-on-time-proof timing subsets separately from all-call planning costs, literal-budget quality/fallback coverage, actual/predicted wins/ties/losses, and strict direction reversals. Same-budget comparisons only. At10/200ms there is one primary pair; at50ms all10 unordered pairs are retained, with the gated method oriented as candidate where applicable.
7. Show the direct subgroup in which all3 policy repetitions used the same plan and activation tick for each arm. Give its actual result and denominator alongside the complete scenario denominator. This subgroup cannot replace the64-case table. It does not make three identical deterministic replay references into independent native replications.
8. A mother-level primary mean averages its predeclared traffic conditions only when all those conditions have known paired physical outcomes. Otherwise retain that mother's missing mean and known-condition count. Full cohort mothers have8 conditions; the pilot has2 conditions and one mother. Technical repetitions and traffic variants do not increase the independent mother count. No inferential interval or power claim is predeclared here.
9. Failed/incomplete native rows retain their original failure ledger and completed/unfinished counts. They are not given zero objective. An interrupted policy matrix fails completeness auditing rather than being silently treated as a complete experiment. All audit attempts use fresh output directories and preserve failures.
10. This stage is one frozen decision epoch, not continuous rolling arrivals. No RAM bytes are reported without separate measurement. The earlier V449 zero-cut/approximately3%-slower diagnostic remains separate and unchanged.

Prepared artifacts: audit_online_raw_v450.py and Preparation_01.json. Prior command-length and AST preparation failures are recorded; neither executed an audit or touched measurement source/raw data. Actual execution waits until root releases the relevant numerical/analysis window.
