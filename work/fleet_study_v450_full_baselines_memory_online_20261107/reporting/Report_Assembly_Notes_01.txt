The retained deployment version is RADIUS_PAIR_AWA_GATED, frozen before this independent comparison. Its added bound represents two incoming, indivisible orders assigned to one receiving vehicle and one transfer site. Existing cargo has an identity, so the same parcel cannot release capacity twice. A common first-handoff time links the two release requirements. The cheap skip test determines whether calculating the added bound can reject the current state. It does not produce a feasible incumbent.

The new independent efficacy cohort contains 64 mother instances at 6, 8, 10 and 12 old orders, each with four capacity/signal conditions. All 256 scenarios and 3,072 solver calls completed. Every method reached the verified optimum within one second in every scenario and repetition. Optimality certification was a separate outcome.

The causal comparison uses the same Hansen-Zhou AWA implementation with and without the new check. Both arms share state transitions, objective, initialization, queue priority, bound cache, Pathmax, deadlines and machine. On their 255 common cases with timely certification in all three repetitions, the added check reduced summed case-median expansions from 170,932 to 77,362 and proof time from 23.1884 to 16.8885 seconds. These correspond to reductions of 54.74% and 27.17%. Among 63 mothers with all four paired conditions certified on time, 60 had lower mean proof time and three had higher mean proof time.

The same candidate reduced proof time by 23.42% and expansions by 51.64% against the complete printed ANA search policy on the same 255 cases. These are complete search-policy reimplementations with declared domain adapters. They do not reproduce the complete Hof-Schneider or Wei freight routing systems. A comparison with those freight systems remains separate work.

The same-kernel candidate reached the exact optimum 7.96% sooner in aggregate across all 256 cases. It reached the 5% and 10% duration-gap thresholds 2.82% and 4.07% later. The gap is measured relative to remaining time from the decision epoch, not relative to an arbitrary absolute clock origin. The evidence supports reduced proof cost and search effort, with a small cost at these earlier quality thresholds.

The specialized frozen deployment and the generic AWA implementation with the new check expanded the same 82,923 states across all 256 cases. The generic implementation took 18.18% longer to finish proof. This engineering difference is reported separately from the contribution of the bound. It is why the frozen deployment remains the selected main version.

Native memory must be described as measured Windows process memory. The immediate post-solve lifetime resident peak includes shared and private resident pages. Private commit is distinct from physical residence and from disk paging. Counts of labels, frontier entries and cache items are not measurements of RAM. The 32-case memory sample comes from the previously frozen efficacy cohort and supplies eight mothers. It is not another independent quality confirmation. Completion and source identity must be checked before inserting measured bytes into the report.

The expanded online experiment uses 64 predefined conditions from eight new mother instances, two receivers, 16 old customer orders and two new indivisible orders. It varies signal offset, pedestrian presence and slow vehicles. It evaluates one frozen decision epoch. The chosen receiving vehicle and transfer site are optimized jointly with its remaining service order. The other vehicle follows the common fixed route. The forecast objective contains all old and new customer flow times. The new lower bound changes only the second new customer's admissible completion term.

Actual native execution is a separate measurement. Vehicles wait during measured planning time and then execute the returned action sequence. The truck stays until the second handoff completes. Actual customer service, truck handoff waiting, road waiting, activation delay and pedestrian exposure remain separate fields. Representative replays follow a predeclared selection rule and must not be called three independent physical repetitions. No actual online advantage is claimed before the complete physical cohort is audited.

The current novelty claim concerns the specific shared-time, identity-preserving capacity bound and its constructive skip condition. Weighted search, cumulative resource thresholds, temporal profiles and lazy heuristic evaluation have prior sources. Formula-level mappings are recorded in the literature matrix. The newly screened COR 107577 paper has only partial accessible primary text. Its complete formula coverage remains unresolved. No universal first-publication claim follows from this search.

All comparisons retain failed checks, soft-limit overruns, slower cases, ties and missing observations. SUMO execution and invariants validate implementation behavior in the tested network. They do not calibrate the network against measured urban traffic.
