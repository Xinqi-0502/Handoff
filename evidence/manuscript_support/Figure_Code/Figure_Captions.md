# Manuscript captions

**Figure 1. Search work and total proof time under a shared search policy.** Hansen–Zhou AWA* (weight 2) with the shared-quota cutoff gate is compared with the same engine using RADIUS alone (reference index 100). Panels show (a) expanded labels and (b) total elapsed time to proof, including initialization and bound work. Each index is 100 times the candidate sum divided by the baseline sum on the identical common cohort; time uses each case's median of three technical repetitions before summation. Both methods must prove optimality within one second in all three repetitions. Eligible pairs are 64/64, 64/64, 64/64 and 63/64 for m=6,8,10,12 old orders; each scale comprises 16 independent mother instances with four related capacity/signal variants. Expanded labels exclude internal bound work. These four tested sizes do not establish asymptotic complexity.

**Figure 2. Endpoint-specific anytime timing tradeoff.** Signed changes are 100 times (candidate time sum / baseline time sum − 1), using the same methods and per-case timing medians as Figure 1. Negative values indicate faster attainment. First gap ≤5%, first gap ≤10%, and first optimum each use 256 common cases; proof uses 255/256. Both methods must reach the stated endpoint within one second in all three repetitions. Gap is (H2−H2*)/(H2*−t0), where H2 is second-handoff completion and H2* the independent optimum. No initial plan met these thresholds. The positive 5% and 10% changes are retained; finding an optimum and proving it are distinct endpoints. These are descriptive ratios of sums, without inferential tests or confidence intervals from correlated variants.

**Figure S1. Native outcomes and the online scope limit.** Panels report (a) actual customer-flow J (sum of customer completion-minus-release durations) and (b) truck handoff waiting excluding two transfer services, for the shared-quota-gated AWA* engine minus its RADIUS-only counterpart. All 64 scenarios, eight independent mothers and all three budgets are retained; every metric/budget has 0 wins, 64 ties, 0 losses and 0 unknowns (tolerance 1e−6 seconds). Case outcomes are policy-frequency-weighted representative native replays, not three independent simulator repeats. **At 10 ms, 192/192 gated and 191/192 baseline policy calls had NO_PLAN_BY_DEADLINE and executed the late common-initial-plan fallback; these are mainly late-fallback physical outcomes, not solutions produced within 10 ms.** Planning latency and activation timing remain charged. This is a single synthetic dispatch epoch, not a calibrated delivery network or continuous online service. The zero physical outcome improvement remains explicit.
